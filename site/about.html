About me

Who I am

Hi, I'm Ellie (Elizabeth if we're being formal)! 🎀 I'm 40, a veteran, and a pup (and sometimes a fox) who loves soft things, pretty ribbons and blinking server lights.

I'm trans, and proud of it. 🏳️‍⚧️

I live with disabilities, and honestly, adapting hasn't been easy. Some days are a struggle. I'm learning to be patient with myself and to build a life that fits who I am now.

Where I'm from

I live in Montréal, Québec. No, I don't speak French… yet. Mais j'essaie! 🥐

What I'm working on

  • Finding my footing: new routines, new hobbies and a new sense of purpose.
  • Reviving old tech: nothing in my homelab is brand new. I give retired machines a second life in my Kubernetes cluster, kintsugi-lab.
  • Becoming a vtuber: getting my model and setup ready to start streaming.
  • Furry art: collecting and sharing art of my pup and fox selves.
  • Learning French, one awkward conversation at a time.

Ribbon Bound Lab is where all of it comes together. Thanks for stopping by. 🐾
