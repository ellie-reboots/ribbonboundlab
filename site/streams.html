My streaming rig ♻️

Just like my homelab, nothing in my streaming PC is brand new. Every single part was bought used and given a second life, and the whole thing cost less than $1,000.

Would I love a shiny new PC? Absolutely. Have you seen prices nowadays, though? 😅 A new graphics card alone costs more than this entire rig.

🖥️ Dell Precision 5820

A retired workstation, reborn as a streaming machine

  • Intel Xeon W-2140B (8 cores). An odd pick, since it's the chip Apple used in the iMac Pro, but it's what was available.
  • 64 GB RAM
  • MSI GeForce RTX 3080 (blower edition)
  • 1.6 TB SSD
  • 1.92 TB SSD

Every part bought used. Total cost: under $1,000.

💡 Why used?

Workstations and graphics cards get retired by businesses and gamers all the time, often with years of life left in them.

Buying used keeps good hardware out of landfills and makes a capable streaming setup affordable. It's the same idea as my homelab: repair, reuse, and give it a second chance.
